Started in a family kitchen. Still cooked like one.

Amara's Kitchen opened in 2019 with four tables and one pot of egusi. Every recipe on our menu started as something cooked for family first — the peppersoup that cured colds, the jollof that showed up at every party, the chin chin that never lasted past the first day.

We still cook in small batches, every morning, with the same spice blends and the same patience. Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is frozen.

— The Amara family
